About

Engineering from first principles to hardware.

I enjoy problems where analytical work, design decisions, and manufacturing constraints all matter—and where the final answer has to survive the real world.

I am pursuing a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Harvard College with a minor in Mathematical Sciences. My coursework includes computer-aided machine design, mechanical systems, solid and computational mechanics, and robotics.

Much of my engineering experience is fabrication-driven. I have worked extensively with manual and CNC machining, welding, 3D printing, laser and waterjet cutting, soldering, CAD/CAE tools, and embedded controls. I especially enjoy projects that require designing, building, instrumenting, and testing a system rather than stopping at a model.

Outside formal coursework, I lead and contribute to rocketry and robotics programs and build personal projects ranging from CNC equipment to vehicle restoration.

Selected Work

Featured projects

A selection of projects spanning propulsion, robotics, controls, mechanical design, manufacturing, simulation, and testing.

01 / PROPULSION

Forward Unto Dawn — Regeneratively Cooled Liquid Bipropellant Engine

Led development work on a regeneratively cooled liquid bipropellant engine featuring an additively manufactured combustion chamber. Work includes mechanical design, propulsion-system integration, manufacturing considerations, and analytical validation using NASA CEA tools.

PropulsionAdditive ManufacturingNASA CEASystems Engineering
02 / ROCKETRY

Mojave Sphinx Rocket + Static Test Stand

Designed, built, and installed the engine test stand at the team's test site, manufactured flight hardware, and led a significant portion of rocket assembly through static-fire operations. The stand was designed for an 800-lbf engine load with a 2× factor of safety.

StructuresMachiningAssemblyStatic Fire
03 / ROBOTICS

University Rover Challenge — Rover Manufacturing

Machined approximately 90% of the rover's custom machined components, including power-transmission parts for the suspension and arm. Also supported process selection and redesign for manufacturability, setup reduction, and practical assembly.

CNC MachiningPower TransmissionDFMRobotics
04 / CONTROLS

Friction & Backlash Compensation Test Platform

Designed a robotics test setup that could introduce controlled variations in friction and backlash, then used experimental data to evaluate their effect on system response and develop a PID controller with friction and backlash compensation.

PID ControlBacklashFrictionExperimental Design
05 / COMPUTATIONAL MECHANICS

Parametric Abaqus Unit-Cell Compression Model

Created a Python-driven parametric Abaqus model for compression testing of a unit cell. Geometry could be varied by number of sides, radius, and height to study how these parameters influence structural response.

AbaqusPythonFEAParametric Modeling
06 / PERSONAL FABRICATION

3-Axis CNC Plasma Cutter

Designed a removable-torch CNC plasma cutting system using NEMA stepper motors and Arduino-based GRBL controls. The machine is structured around an off-the-shelf plasma torch that can be installed in or removed from the CNC carriage.

GRBLArduinoNEMA MotorsCNC
