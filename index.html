About Me

Engineering from first principles to hardware.

I’m most interested in work where analytical reasoning, design decisions, manufacturing constraints, and physical testing all matter.

I am pursuing a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Harvard College with a minor in Mathematical Sciences. My coursework includes computer-aided machine design, mechanical systems, solid and computational mechanics, and robotics.

My experience is strongly fabrication-driven: manual and CNC machining, welding, 3D printing, laser and waterjet cutting, soldering, CAD/CAE, embedded controls, fixture design, testing, and iterative build work.

Outside coursework, I lead and contribute to rocketry and robotics programs, work in research environments, and build personal projects ranging from CNC equipment to vehicle restoration.
